§ 01 · The Brief

What we audit.

Six surfaces of attack. One coordinated investigation. Each subagent carries a focused system prompt and a small, deterministic toolset — local for development, MCP for production. Mutating tools are filtered out by default.

01  /  06  ·  supply-chain
SUBAGENT · 01 / 06
α

supply-chain

Hunts typosquats, malicious post-install hooks, import-time secret harvesting, and outbound exfil patterns in vendored or installed packages. The first line of defense before anything reaches your build.

typosquat-scan post-install exfil-trace vendor-diff
SUBAGENT · 02 / 06
β

cicd-monitoring

Inspects GitHub Actions and Jenkins workflows for unsafe step composition, suspicious build activity, and possible secret exposure in logs. Tracks who pushed what, when, and from where.

gha-lint jenkins-audit log-redact runner-trust
SUBAGENT · 03 / 06
γ

access-control

Reviews IAM policies for over-privileged principals, MFA enforcement, stale service-account keys, and crossed account boundaries. Least privilege, restored — without a thirty-page ticket.

iam-graph mfa-check key-age priv-esc
SUBAGENT · 04 / 06
δ

config-audit

Sweeps Dockerfiles, Kubernetes manifests, and Terraform with CIS Benchmark and Checkov-style rules. Catches the misconfiguration before the misconfiguration catches you.

cis-bench checkov k8s-policy tf-plan
SUBAGENT · 05 / 06
ε

code-analysis

SAST-grade review for hardcoded secrets, injection paths, deserialization gadgets, and quietly insecure coding patterns. Reads your repo the way an attacker reads your repo.

sast-core secret-grep injection taint-flow
SUBAGENT · 06 / 06
ζ

dependency-security

SCA across npm audit, PyPI safety, and SBOM-style review. CVE matching, transitive risk surfacing, and version drift — explained in language a release manager will actually read.

cve-match npm-audit pypi-safety sbom
§ 02 · The Counsel

One orchestrator.
Six specialists.

agent.py builds a single DeepAgents orchestrator that delegates to six subagents. Local tools live under tools/ with deterministic demo behavior. tools/mcp.py optionally loads real MCP tools at startup — mutating tools filtered by default.

DeepAgents Orchestrator · agent.py
α · 01 supply-chain Typosquats, install hooks, exfil patterns.
β · 02 cicd-monitoring GHA, Jenkins, secret leakage in logs.
γ · 03 access-control IAM, MFA, stale keys, priv-esc.
δ · 04 config-audit Docker, K8s, Terraform, CIS.
ε · 05 code-analysis SAST, secrets, injection, taint.
ζ · 06 dependency-security CVE, npm/PyPI, SBOM, drift.
